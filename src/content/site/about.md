Helen Zhang is an artist based in New York.

BFA in Fine Arts from Parsons School of Design, with a minor in Creative Coding. Background in painting and engineering.

Current work is focused in installation, moving image, spaces, and technology.

Especially interested in making as a method of processing and responding to the times; and in technology — in use and conceptually — in regard to psychology, ethics, and human response.
